Fatto a mano · made by hand

From flour to fork

Every sheet of pasta at Bontà is made in our kitchen. This is how a plate of ravioli happens.

  1. Roll

    Egg dough is rolled out into long, silky golden sheets.

  2. Fill

    Spinach and ricotta is piped on in neat little mounds.

  3. Cover

    A second sheet goes over the top and is pressed down around every mound.

  4. Cut

    Each raviolo is cut with a fluted cutter, ready for the pan and your plate.
