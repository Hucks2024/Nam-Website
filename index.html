Our food

A taste of the menu

Some of our favourites. The menu changes with the seasons, so ask what's good today.

    Pizza with prosciutto, rocket and a whole burrata.

  • Funghi e salsiccia

    White pizza with mushrooms, sausage and burrata.

  • Ravioli al pesto

    Handmade spinach and ricotta ravioli in basil pesto.
